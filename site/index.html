Latest year vs the reference normal ?Daily mean temperature for the latest year (red) against the chosen reference period. Grey bands are ±1σ and ±2σ of daily values in that period; the coral band is the latest year's daily max–min range.

Warming stripes ?One stripe per year, coloured by the annual mean temperature minus the reference-period average. Blue is cooler, red is warmer than the baseline.

Monthly temperature anomaly ?Each cell is that month's average daily mean minus the same calendar month's average over the reference period — a difference of monthly averages. Red means warmer than normal.

Daily temperature heatmap ?One row per year and one column per day; colour is the selected metric. The month slider narrows the part of the year shown.
